## Questions I’ve Spent Time Investigating

I enjoy projects where implementation is only half the work. The other half is checking whether the result actually supports the claim.

### Agreement Without Faithfulness
*Does high attribution agreement between explainability frameworks mean the explanations are faithful to the underlying model?*

Using TreeSHAP, LIME, and permutation sensitivity with an XGBoost classifier, this study compares attribution agreement with comprehensiveness and sufficiency. The reported results show negligible-to-small relationships, suggesting that agreement alone is not enough to establish faithfulness.

[Manuscript (PDF)](https://github.com/Angat-Shah/xai-agreement-without-faithfulness/blob/main/paper/manuscript.pdf) · [Evaluation Pipeline Code](https://github.com/Angat-Shah/xai-agreement-without-faithfulness)

### Simulated quantum randomness
*Can simulated quantum measurements produce bitstreams with the statistical characteristics expected of an ideal random source?*

The QRNG work evaluates 10,000 generated bits using frequency, Shannon entropy, chi-square, and lag-1 autocorrelation measures, while documenting the limitations of drawing conclusions about physical quantum entropy from a simulator.

[Research Report](https://github.com/Angat-Shah/QRNG/blob/main/docs/RESEARCH_REPORT.md) · [Code & Streamlit Analyzer](https://github.com/Angat-Shah/QRNG)

## Principles I Build By

I’m drawn to a few habits:

- **Build to understand.** A question becomes clearer when there’s something concrete to test.
- **Question the consensus.** A working demo is a start; it isn’t evidence for every claim around it.
- **Make the result usable.** Good work should be understandable to the next person, not just the person who wrote it.
- **Leave something behind.** Sometimes that’s a product. Sometimes it’s a report, a tool, or a repository that helps someone else move faster.

I’m interested in building reliable software and understanding the ideas behind intelligent systems, especially machine learning and the data that shapes their behaviour.
